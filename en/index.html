Why Mulligan

A club without a clubhouse. But with rivals.

  • Every round counts

    Your Mulligan Rating changes after every scored round. What counts is who you beat, not by how many strokes. No waiting for the next tournament to see if you're getting better.

  • Competition without entry fees

    Everyone vs everyone or 2 vs 2, with scores confirmed by the flight. The app is free. You only pay your green fee on site.

  • New people at your level

    Every flight brings people who play like you. Plus range nights and events where playing partners become friends. Or more.

  • Show what you've got

    Your profile shows your rank, rating, personal best and milestones. About five wins against equally strong players move you up a rank, from Rookie to Albatross.

How it works

One tap and you're in the flight.

Every match is a 9-hole round for two to four players. Whoever opens the match books the tee time with the club. Everyone pays their green fee on site.

  1. 01Find

    Find or open a match

    Open matches nearby with club, tee time and game mode. You see who's playing and at what level before you join. Or open one yourself and invite friends.

  2. 02Play

    Enter your score

    After the round, everyone enters their strokes and another player confirms them. Where to meet and who's driving gets sorted in the flight chat beforehand.

  3. 03Climb

    Rating and rank

    Win and you gain points, more against stronger players. That way your next matches put you with people who fit your game.

  4. 04Connect

    Friends, clubs and events

    Follow people from your flight, give applause for good rounds and discover clubs, range nights and events near you.
