Become a partner

Show up where flights come together.

Mulligan brings together golfers all over Germany who want to play more and compete against people at their level. As a partner, you host events with us, show off your club or present your product where the next round gets planned.

Send a partner request

Or write directly to contact@mulligan-playgolf.com

Who it's for

For everyone who makes golf happen.

  • Golf clubs and courses

    Range nights, taster days and open tournaments at your place. Players see your news and the open matches at your course in the app.

  • Indoor and simulators

    Simulator nights, winter dates and indoor tournaments. That keeps your facility on people's minds in the off-season too.

  • Coaches

    Taster lessons and courses as events. Booking lessons directly in the app is in the works. You can already sign up for it.

  • Brands and products

    Test days, goodies and sponsored events. Your name is on the event, labelled as sponsor.

What you get

Your event in the app. Your people on the course.

  • Events people see

    We create your event in the app with title, place, time, spots, description and image. It appears in the events list, as a card in the feed and on the day itself in the match search. Joining and tickets in the app will follow.

  • Visibility for your club

    Partner clubs get their own description, images and news in the app. Anyone who marks your club as a favourite sees your latest news right on the home screen.

  • Clearly labelled, no data trading

    Sponsored events carry the note "Sponsored by" with your name. We do not pass on users' personal data to partners. After the event you get anonymous numbers, for example how many people joined.

How it works

Three steps into the app.

  1. 01Request

    Write to us

    Tell us briefly who you are and what you have in mind: an event, a club profile or a product.

  2. 02Talk

    We plan together

    We sort out the idea, date, setup and terms.

  3. 03Live

    Into the app

    We create the event or club profile and agree everything with you beforehand.
