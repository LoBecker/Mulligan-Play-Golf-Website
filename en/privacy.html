This privacy policy applies to the website mulligan-playgolf.com and to the Mulligan iOS app. It has five parts: A. General, B. Website, C. App, D. Your rights and E. Changes.

The short version

  • We only process the data we need so you can find players, play matches and get your rating.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. The website sets no cookies. We do not sell data.
  • Our database is located in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Some service providers are based outside the EU. Contractual safeguards are in place for them.
  • All signed-in users can see your profile, your matches with place, time and playing partners, and your rating history with placements, including for past matches. Photos, subtitles and descriptions of your rounds are only visible to people who follow you, and only if you publish them. Only the people involved can read messages.
  • Your location stays on your iPhone.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app.
  • Where we process data based on legitimate interests, you can object (see Right to object).

Legal bases

We only process personal data where one of these legal bases applies. Each processing activity below states which one.

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): we need the data to provide Mulligan to you under our terms of use or to handle a request before a contract is concluded.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): for example obligations under the Digital Services Act (DSA) when handling notices, or duties to provide information to authorities.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): we balance our interest against yours. We state our interest in each case. You can object to this processing (see Right to object).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): only where we expressly ask for it. At present this only applies to notifications about new events. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.
  • Access to your device (Section 25 TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act): the app stores or reads information on your iPhone only where strictly necessary for a feature you explicitly use (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
  • iOS permissions: for location, calendar and notifications, iOS also asks for your permission. These permissions are settings on your iPhone, not consent within the meaning of the GDPR. You can change them at any time in iOS settings.

When you email us

Security

All connections between the app, the website and our servers are encrypted (TLS). Supabase stores data encrypted on its storage media. Every table in our database is protected by access rules so that each person only sees the data they are allowed to see. Only the two partners have administrative access. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

B. Website

Hosting and server logs

Our website is hosted by Vercel (details in the table of service providers). When you visit the website, Vercel processes technically necessary data:

  • IP address and the country and city derived from it
  • date and time of the request
  • page requested, status code and amount of data transferred
  • browser, operating system and the previously visited page, if your browser sends them

This is necessary to show you the website, fend off attacks and find errors. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a secure and working website. We can view these logs for at most one day. Beyond that, Vercel keeps logs only as long as needed for operations and security.

No cookies, no tracking, no external fonts

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. We use no analytics or advertising services. Fonts are served from our own server, so your browser does not load anything from third parties. There is no contact form.

Links to other sites

The website contains links, for example to the App Store or to OpenStreetMap. You only leave our website when you tap a link. The linked site's own privacy policy then applies.

C. App

Download from the App Store

When you download Mulligan from the App Store, Apple processes the data required for this, such as your Apple ID and the time of download. We have no influence on this. From Apple we only receive aggregated figures, such as how often the app was downloaded, without any link to you.

Sign-in and account

You sign in with your email address and a one-time code, or with Sign in with Apple.

Account. We store your email address and the time of your last sign-in. Supabase logs every sign-in with IP address and user agent, meaning technical information about the app and operating system. Supabase deletes these logs after 7 days. Right after your first sign-in, you create your profile. If you don't, we delete your sign-in data after 30 days.

Sign-in with email code. We send the code via Resend. For this, Resend receives your email address and the content of the email and keeps the email and sending logs for 30 days, and up to 7 days longer in backups. A code is only valid for a short time and works only once.

Sign in with Apple. Apple confirms your identity to us. We receive an identifier that only applies to Mulligan, and your email address. If you choose "Hide My Email", we receive a relay address from Apple. On your first sign-in, Apple may also send us your name. We only store the first name and the first letter of the last name as a suggestion for your profile. We also store an Apple token in encrypted form. We only need it to revoke the link between Mulligan and your Apple ID when you delete your account.

Session. While you are signed in, Supabase stores the IP address, a technical identifier of the app (user agent) and timestamps for your session. We delete this data when you sign out or delete your account.

Server logs. Every request from the app to our backend creates technical logs with IP address, time, function called and result. Supabase deletes them after 7 days.

Emails from the app. We only send what your account requires: sign-in codes, replies to notices, decisions about your content or account, notices of changed terms and before deletion due to inactivity. We send no newsletter and no advertising.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. For the Apple token and the logs, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR also applies. Our legitimate interest is deleting your account completely when you ask us to, including the link to your Apple ID, and detecting and fending off attacks on accounts, such as mass guessing of codes.

Profile

Right after your first sign-in, you create a profile. First name, last name and date of birth are required. In addition, you enter your home town and, if you like, your home club and a photo. We also store the app language, when you accepted which version of the terms of use, and your Mulligan Rating, which starts at 1000.

We use your date of birth to check that you are at least 18. The app calculates your age from it, for example for a match's age range. Only you and we can see your full date of birth, others only see your year of birth and age. Once your profile is created, it can only be changed through support.

Who sees what: all signed-in users can see your first and last name, your year of birth and age, your home town, your home club, your photo, your rating and rank, your stats (rounds, courses, followers, following) and your badges. Other users can find you in the player search by your first name, last name and username. Under "Find friends", the app suggests you to others: people from shared flights, friends of your friends and players with the same home club, if they also show their home club on their profile. Others do not see the home course that sets your region and country in the leaderboards. Only upload a profile photo that shows you and no one else.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep the data until you change it or delete your account. A replaced photo is deleted immediately.

Matches, flights and invitations

When you open a match, we store the club, date, time, game mode, flight size, the age range if you set one, meeting point, minutes before, whether the tee time is reserved, and your note. When you join a match, we store that you are in the flight and, for 2 vs 2, your team.

When you join, the database automatically checks whether your profile is complete, whether your age fits the range, whether a spot is free and whether you have a block with anyone in the flight.

Who sees what: all signed-in users can see open, full and already played matches, including the people in the flight with their first and last name, photo, age, rank and rating. So others can see when, where and with whom you plan to play or have played. Only the people in the flight can see cancelled matches. Invitations are visible to the invited person and the flight.

The host books the tee time with the club personally. We do not pass any data to clubs.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Played matches are kept as long as at least one person from the flight still has an account, so results and ratings remain traceable. We delete cancelled matches 30 days after the planned start time, and invitations 30 days after the match start time.

Match chat and direct messages

In the match chat you write with the people in the flight. There are also system messages, for example who joined or who entered a score. Only people currently in the flight can read the match chat.

Direct messages are exchanged with exactly one other person. Only the two of you can read them. If one of you blocks the other, neither of you can see the conversation any more.

We store the text, sender and time of each message. Messages are encrypted in transit but not end-to-end encrypted. We do not read your messages. Before a message is saved, it is automatically checked against the word list described under Reporting, blocking and moderation. No person reads along. We only see messages in two cases: if someone reports a message, we see the reported message and the description in the notice. We also disclose messages where we are legally required to.

When you delete one of your messages, we remove the text immediately. The chat then only shows that a message was deleted. We delete match chat messages 12 months after the match start time. Direct messages are kept until you delete them or one of you deletes their account. We then delete the whole conversation.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Scores and Mulligan Rating

After the match you enter your gross strokes and, if you like, Stableford points, putts and fairways hit. A playing partner confirms your entry or disputes it with a reason. Without a dispute, an entry counts as confirmed after 48 hours. Entries, confirmations and disputes are only visible to the people in the flight.

From confirmed results, the database automatically calculates your Mulligan Rating according to fixed rules. You can find the rules in the app under Profile, "How your Mulligan Rating works". We store your current rating, your best rating, the number of rated rounds and, for each rated match, the rating before and after, the change and your placement.

Who sees what: all signed-in users can see your rating, your rank and your rating history with placements.

The rating evaluates your playing strength compared with others. This is profiling within the meaning of Art. 4(4) GDPR. We only use it to make well-matched flights possible, for example for the "Fits me" filter, and for ranks and milestones. The rating does not decide whether you may join a match and has no legal effect on you. If we find that results were manipulated, we may correct the ratings concerned.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. This also applies to corrections in case of manipulation or technical errors, which we agreed in Section 5(4) of the terms of use. Where a correction also affects other players, we additionally rely on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest, and that of the other players, is a rating that is not distorted by false results. We keep the data until you delete your account.

Solo rounds

Rounds you play on your own can be added as a solo round, up to three months back. We store the course, holes, date and time, your strokes and, if you enter them, putts and fairways hit per hole. Solo rounds only count towards your own stats, not towards the Mulligan Rating, leaderboards or milestones. There are no playing partners.

Who sees what: only you can see your solo rounds. If you share a solo round in the feed, your followers see the post with club and strokes, marked as a solo round. When you played is not part of the post.

Deleting: you can delete any solo round in your profile. We then delete it together with its post, photo, applause and comments.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep solo rounds until you delete them or your account.

Clubs and distances

In the Clubs tab of your stats you can record which clubs are in your bag and add their distances, as carry, total or both. We store the clubs, each distance with its date and source (range, simulator, course or manual) so you can see the history, and your settings: unit, how often the app reminds you of a distance check, and whether your bag appears on your profile.

Who sees what: only you can see your clubs and distances. If you turn on "Show on profile", all signed-in users can see your clubs with their latest distance under "My bag" on your profile, without history or source. Anyone blocked with you sees nothing. The app schedules the reminder as a notification on your iPhone, nothing is sent to us for it.

Deleting: you can remove clubs from your bag in the tab and delete single distances in a club's history.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep the data until you delete it or your account.

Feed, follows, posts, photos and comments

Following. You can follow others without their approval. Likewise, any signed-in user can follow you. All signed-in users can see whom you follow and who follows you.

Rounds, milestones, rank-ups. After each rated match, a post with club, strokes, placement and rating change is created. Milestones and rank-ups are added too. After each round, the app asks you whether the round should appear in the feed. Until you agree, only you can see it. You can change this later for each individual post. Published posts are visible to everyone who follows you. Unpublished posts are visible only to you. Your followers can see how many rounds you played in the last seven days under "This week".

Photos, subtitles and descriptions. You can add a photo, subtitle and description to a round. Before the app uploads a photo, it resizes it to at most 1600 pixels and saves it again. This removes all metadata, including where the photo was taken. Photos are kept in non-public storage and can only be retrieved by people who are allowed to see the post. Only upload photos that everyone shown in them has agreed to.

Applause and comments. Anyone who can see a post can applaud and, for published rounds, comment. Applause and comments are visible to everyone who can see the post. You can delete your own comments. The person who created the post can delete all comments under it.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep posts, photos, comments, applause and follows until you remove them or delete your account. Deleted photos and comments are removed immediately.

Clubs, favourites and club reviews

Club data comes from OpenStreetMap. It contains no data about you. Only you can see your favourite clubs ("My clubs").

You can only review a club if you have played at least one rated match there. The database checks this automatically. There is one review from you per club. A new review replaces the old one. Your review with stars, text, first and last name and photo is visible to all signed-in users, including people who work for a club.

If a club complains about your review, we examine the complaint. We send you the complaint and ask for your comments. We do not give your contact details to the club. We only disclose subscriber data under a court order (Section 21 TDDDG).

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and for examining complaints also Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 16 DSA and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is removing unlawful reviews and protecting lawful ones. We keep reviews and favourites until you delete them or your account.

Events

When you join or leave an event, we store this. All signed-in users can see how many people are going and, for up to five people, their first name and photo. So after you join, your first name and photo may appear there. We do not pass your attendance on to sponsors or clubs. If an event says "Sponsored by", a partner supports the event. The partner receives no data from us.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep event attendance until you delete your account.

Reporting, blocking and moderation

Reporting. You can report people, matches, events, messages, direct messages, posts, comments and reviews. We store who reported, what was reported, the reason, your description and the time. We are notified of new reports by an email sent via Resend that contains these details. People without an account can report content by email. We then process their name, email address and the details of the notice. We do not tell the reported person who reported them, unless this is strictly necessary for the decision and legally permitted.

Moderation. We review reports ourselves and decide as humans. If we remove content or restrict an account, we store the decision with reasons and inform you under Art. 17 DSA. The person who reported learns how we decided (Art. 16 DSA). A word list automatically checks texts before they are saved. If a text contains a word on the list, it is not saved and you see a notice. If there is a suspicion of a criminal offence threatening the life or safety of persons, we inform the competent authorities (Art. 18 DSA).

Blocking. Only you can see whom you have blocked. However, the blocked person will notice, for example, that they can no longer message you.

The legal bases are Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 16 to 18 DSA, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a safe and respectful community. We delete reports and decisions 12 months after they are closed. For a suspension, we keep the decision until 12 months after the suspension ends. We keep blocks until you lift them or delete your account.

Feedback

When you give feedback, we store your satisfaction from 1 to 5 and, if you provide them, type, area and text. App version, iOS version, device type and language are sent automatically. You can see these details in the form before you send it. Feedback is linked to your account so we can ask follow-up questions about bugs. Only we can read it.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is understanding the bugs and requests you send us and getting back to you with questions. We delete feedback after 24 months, at the latest when your account is deleted.

Notifications (push)

The app asks for permission to send notifications at the end of setting up your account, if at least one type of notification is turned on, and when you choose a reminder in the Clubs tab. If you allow them, we store a device identifier for notifications (push token) and whether it belongs to a test or store version of the app. You get notifications, for example, when someone joins your match, messages you or your flight, or confirms your score. If you mute a match chat, we store that so you don't get notifications for it.

Notifications are delivered via the Apple Push Notification service. Apple receives the push token and the content of the notification. The content may include names, the place and time of a match and, for messages, their text. Whether notifications appear with content on the lock screen is set in iOS. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS settings.

New events. We only notify you about new events if you turn on the "New events" switch under Profile, Settings, Notifications. It is off at the start. The legal basis for this is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by turning the switch off.

The legal basis for all other notifications is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and, for the push token, Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG. We delete the push token when you sign out or delete your account, and as soon as Apple tells us it is no longer valid, for example because you deleted the app.

Location and place search

The app only asks for your location when you use "Discover" on the home screen or "Current location" in the place picker, and only while you are using the app. It uses an approximate location to sort clubs, matches and events by distance and to filter by radius. Your location stays on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to third parties and is not stored. Without permission, the app sorts from the home course in your profile or from a place you choose yourself. If neither is set, it sorts by name. You can withdraw permission at any time in iOS settings.

You can also search for a place manually. For this, the app uses Apple's MapKit map service. Apple receives your search text and your iPhone's IP address and processes them under its own responsibility. The app stores the chosen place only on your iPhone.

The legal basis is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and, insofar as Apple receives data for the place search, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Calendar

With "Add to calendar", the app adds a match or event to your calendar. It only asks for permission to add events. It cannot read your calendar. The entry contains title, place, time and meeting point. It then sits in your calendar and is synced as you have set up in iOS. We receive nothing from it. The legal basis is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

Access to photos

You choose photos using the iOS photo picker. The app only sees the photos you select, not your whole library. A photo is only uploaded when you save it as your profile photo or in a post.

Storage on your iPhone

The app only stores on your iPhone what its features need: your sign-in in the iOS keychain, your filters, the manually chosen place with radius, which messages you have read, score cards of matches and solo rounds you have started until you save them, the unit for club distances, the scheduled distance check reminder, and cached images. This data does not leave your iPhone. When you sign out or delete your account, the app removes your sign-in and these settings. The legal basis is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

Crash reports and TestFlight

We use no tools of our own for crash or usage reports. If you have allowed iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple passes crash reports and technical metrics on to us. They contain the device type, iOS version and technical details of the crash, but nothing that lets us identify you directly. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is fixing bugs. You control sharing in iOS under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

If you take part in a test phase via Apple's TestFlight, we receive your name and email address from the invitation from Apple, as well as feedback and screenshots you send via TestFlight. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We delete this data at the latest 6 months after the test phase ends.

No tracking, no ads

Mulligan contains no advertising and no third-party tools for analytics, tracking or advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites. That is why the app does not ask for permission to track.

Deleting your account and inactivity

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile, Settings, "Delete account". We then immediately delete:

  • your profile with photo, your rating and your rating history
  • your scores, round posts with photos, subtitles and descriptions, your milestones
  • your comments and all comments under your posts, your applause
  • your club reviews, favourites, follows and followers, blocks
  • your messages in match chats and all your direct messages with the conversations
  • your participation in matches. Matches you opened that have not yet started are deleted together with their chat. Played matches remain for the other people in the flight, without any link to you.
  • invitations sent and received, event attendance, feedback and push tokens
  • your sign-in data. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the link to your Apple ID.

What remains: other players' ratings stay as they resulted from shared matches. Reports you sent are kept without any link to you until their retention period ends. If a report or suspension concerns your account, we keep the decision until its retention period ends. Backups still contain deleted data for up to 7 days, logs for up to 7 days. Emails and sending logs at Resend remain for up to 30 days, and up to 7 days longer in backups.

Inactivity. If you have not used Mulligan for 24 months, we will email you. If you then neither open the app while signed in nor sign in within 4 weeks, we delete your account as described above.

Do you have to provide data?

For an account you need an email address or an Apple ID. Before you can use Mulligan, you create a profile with first name and year of birth and accept the terms of use. Without these details you cannot use Mulligan. Everything else is optional.

Automated decisions

Only one decision is based solely on automated processing and significantly affects you (Art. 22 GDPR): if your year of birth shows that you are under 18, the database does not create a profile and no user agreement is concluded. This is necessary for entering into the contract (Art. 22(2)(a) GDPR). If you think this is wrong, write to contact@mulligan-playgolf.com. A human will then review it, and you can state your point of view and contest the decision. Other rule-based checks also run automatically but do not have such an effect: whether you can join a match or review a club, the rating calculation and the word list for texts. If you think the result of such a check is wrong, write to us. A person will then look at the case. Reports, suspensions and terminations are always decided by humans.

D. Your rights

You have the following rights against us:

  • Access to the data we process about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of incorrect data (Art. 16 GDPR). You can change most details yourself in your profile.
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR). You can delete your account yourself in the app.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability: you receive data you provided to us in a common, machine-readable format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR), for example for notifications about new events. The iOS permissions for location, calendar and notifications are not consent within the meaning of the GDPR. You change them in iOS settings.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where we process data based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop processing this data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

How to exercise your rights

For all your rights and your objection, write to contact@mulligan-playgolf.com, ideally from your account's email address. So that we do not give data to the wrong person, we may ask you to confirm that you are the account holder. We reply within one month.
