Section 7 Community rules

Mulligan works because everyone plays fair and treats each other with respect. These rules apply in the app, in the chat, in direct messages, in the feed, in reviews and on the course at matches and events arranged through Mulligan.

How we play

  • Be on time at the meeting point and on the tee.
  • Cancel early if you cannot come.
  • Count honestly and only confirm what is correct.
  • Play at a good pace and let faster flights through.
  • Respect the club's rules, the course and the club's staff.
  • Treat everyone fairly, however well they play.

Not allowed

  • insults, harassment, bullying, threats and intimidation
  • hate, incitement and discrimination, for example because of origin, skin colour, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age
  • sexual content, sexual harassment and advances after someone has said no
  • depictions of violence, glorification of violence and calls to commit crimes
  • publishing photos, names, addresses, phone numbers or other data of other people without their consent
  • false identities, fake profiles, multiple accounts, pretending to be someone else, including a club or Mulligan
  • false scores, arranged confirmations and invented matches to influence ratings or reviews
  • advertising, spam, chain messages and commercial offers
  • content you do not hold the rights to, for example other people's photos
  • malware, attacks on our systems, automated access such as bots or scraping, and circumventing suspensions or blocks
  • repeatedly not showing up to matches without cancelling
  • any other content or conduct that breaks the law

Blocking

You can block anyone. Blocking works both ways: you can no longer join the same match, invite each other, follow each other, send each other direct messages or comment on each other's posts. You no longer see the other person's messages, posts, comments and reviews. You can lift blocks under Profile, Settings, "Blocked people".

Test profiles during the test phase

As long as Mulligan is not on the App Store, the test version contains three made-up test profiles run by us. They open matches and join each other's matches so that there are open matches to test with. No real person is behind them. They do not reply in the chat and do not confirm scores. The ban on fake profiles does not apply to them. They do not exist in the App Store version.
